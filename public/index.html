Before you start

The clock runs

Each section starts as soon as it loads and submits itself when the time is gone. Hide the clock if it distracts you; the section still ends on time.

Print the report

Every report has a Print / Save as PDF button. The printout carries the questions, what you answered and the answer key, with the grammar note or the band-5 model for the writing tasks. Choose “Save as PDF” as the destination to keep a copy, or press Ctrl‑P.

Nothing is saved for you

Answers live in the browser tab and disappear when you close it. Print the report before you leave, and hand the PDF to your teacher if that is what was asked for.

No mouse needed

Tab moves between controls, Enter picks a word up and places it, and Esc cancels. Everything works on a phone or tablet too.

A fresh set every time

Questions are drawn at random from the banks, so a second sitting is a different paper. The writing prompts change with it.

What is scored

Reading and Build a Sentence are marked right or wrong automatically. The email and the discussion cannot be machine scored, so the report runs the mechanical checks, shows a band-5 model and leaves the rubric to you.
