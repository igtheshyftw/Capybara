Adaptive Reading Practice

An adaptive practice test that adjusts question difficulty to the reader as they go. Questions come from a fixed local item bank; nothing is sent over the network.

This is a practice adaptive score and is not an official NWEA RIT score. This tool is not affiliated with NWEA and is not MAP Growth. It approximates publicly described computer-adaptive testing principles; it is not NWEA's proprietary production engine.
